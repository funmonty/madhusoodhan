What I do

I'm a Senior Technical Lead in computer vision and machine learning. My career has moved through three companies in the CV/ML space — starting on stereo-vision and embedded products at e-con Systems, then bootstrapping an ML team at Mobiveil, and now leading CV/ML work at MulticoreWare. End-to-end ownership has been the constant: from early problem framing and proposal writing to deployment, customer releases, and long-term maintenance.

Alongside the engineering, I run the team side of the work: appraisals, goal-setting, bandwidth planning, and live technical reviews. I've managed 15–20 engineers across the years, typically holding a team of 5–6 at a time across projects and seniority levels. I've also mentored intern cohorts through industry–academia collaborations on a 3D conveyor-belt CV/VR project and on EvoPrunerPool.

Beyond delivery, I'm active on the business side: writing SOWs and proposals, partnering with business units, and supporting presales. I've led two internal research projects through to submission within MulticoreWare's research wing — one of which became my US patent on Human 3D pose estimation for gait analysis.
